How Ham Radio shaped my future and got me a job in web development

When I was about 10 years old, I became interested in trainspotting and radio scanning combined with good IT skills (I liked experimenting with computers, OSs, the Internet, some photography, gaming, etc.).

As I got more into it, I discovered the world of ham radio, met passionate people in SP8KKM and SP8POP clubs (including: Robert SP8TLH, Paweł SO8FM, Krzysztof SQ8ERS, Damian SP8GMR, Piotr SQ8L, Piotr SP8MRD, Ryszard SP8RD), and passed my C-class exam in 2015.

In 2017, I got an opportunity to develop a modern website for the SP8POP club with a refreshing design compared to many ham websites — and that’s how I became interested in frontend development & related fields (design etc.), which I now do for a living! (you can see my portfolio website: kamilpawlak.com)

Also after I got to the “creative” side of things, I quickly discovered the magic of Apple systems (which are the industry standard for frontend & design) after being frustrated with Windows web development limitations and Android phones that were laggy even with mods. Inspired by Piotr SP8MRD’s use of iPhone, I made a switch to iOS in 2019 and to Mac in 2020. I try to use Apple systems whenever possible, I can’t imagine using Windows and Android for my daily & business tasks. And I like the RUMlogNG Mac logger, it’s the perfect and well-designed solution for me.

And in 2024, after a long break caused by school & career development, I returned to the hobby and passed my full A-class license.

So, for me, amateur radio is the passion that led to my personal & career development and I’m grateful to be a ham. It just perfectly shaped my future! Without it, I definitely wouldn’t be where I am now.

PZK POLSKA Ham Radio Award PDF Generator

2026

A modern, responsive React web application that completely automates the tedious process of filling out the official "POLSKA" Award PDF forms issued by the PZK (Polish Amateur Radio Union).

Instead of manually fighting with interactive PDF forms or filling them by hand and stitching together, ham radio operators can use this web UI to punch in their QSO contacts, select their award categories, and automatically generate a flattened, print-ready PDF containing both their Application and required Record Sheets.

The form fills out the PDF locally in the browser using pdf-lib (no server backend required), automatically giving full data protection compliance.

React Vite shadcn/ui react-hook-form pdf-lib
